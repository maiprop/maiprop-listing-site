## Behavior and cache contract

`useFavoriteMembership` reads the six favorite-row fields (`id, user_id, listing_id, price_alert, price_when_saved, created_at`) once per actor, ordered by saved date. `useIsFavorited` and the membership-only Auctions, PropertyDetail, OpenHouseDetail, NewBuildDetail, and PropertyCard surfaces use that row cache. `useFavorites` retains the full joined listing output, including null `listings` for inaccessible/deleted public listings, for Dashboard, Portfolios, and PortfoliosTab. It fetches the same favorite-row query through QueryClient and only then hydrates public listing details. Thus a cold full view still has a dependent hydration request; the large listing transfer has moved off ordinary heart-only routes. These are distinct array-shaped, actor-scoped keys: `["favoriteMembership", userId]` and `["favorites", userId]`. No Set or cross-user seed is stored in either cache.

Toggle operations capture `operationUserId` at invocation. They cancel the two exact actor keys, apply an optimistic change to membership and an existing full cache, and restore only the failed listing's prior row and position. Distinct overlapping mutations therefore retain another listing's confirmed change when failure and success settle in either order. Both actor keys invalidate after the last overlapping write settles if any write succeeded, avoiding a refetch that overwrites another pending optimistic change. Undefined prior cache returns to absence after an isolated failure; no actor makes no read or optimistic cache. Price alert and saved price remain on favorite rows. A full cache's optimistic new row has `listings: null` until the successful refetch hydrates it.

## Independent-review fix round 1 — pending save and late full hydration

Review of `95ae5e87d1c7130ae118b7fd622561de90c245d6` demonstrated that a full saved view mounted during a pending save could retain the optimistic favorite after the save failed. The earlier rollback only touched a full cache that existed at `onMutate`. It also allowed an in-flight hydration to publish that failed row after rollback.

Fix commit: `0a72470c5849a9887e5105803a0c8848319ca1f5`. Failure recovery now cancels the captured actor's exact full query before restoring the membership row, removes only the failed listing from a full cache that exists at failure time, and invalidates the exact full key so an active view rederives from corrected membership. A cancelled late hydration cannot publish its captured optimistic row. The restoration remains listing-specific, preserving another pending or confirmed listing. This can cause an additional full-view hydration after a failed write while that view is active; it is necessary to recover from a cancelled derived query. The heart-only path does not acquire a hydration RPC.
